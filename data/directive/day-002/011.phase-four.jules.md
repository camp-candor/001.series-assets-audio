#### 1. Specialized Terminal Cockpit Modalities (`packages/701.assetsaudio/98.menu.unit/buz/`)

Implement the five specialized cockpit modality buzzers and wire them into `98.menu.unit/menu.action.ts`, `menu.buzzer.ts`, `menu.reduce.ts`, and `buz/00.menu.buzz.ts`:

1. **`auditionTake.buzz.ts` (`auditionVoiceTake`)**:
   - Inspects the target `.wav` path in `voices/versions/` or `voices/active/`, verifies RIFF/WAVE header integrity, calculates playback duration in milliseconds, and dispatches `>> [AUDITION:PLAY] Character=${characterSlug} File=${wavPath} Duration=${durationMs}ms (48kHz/24-bit)` to `cns00`.
2. **`timbreComparison.buzz.ts` (`compareVoiceTimbre`)**:
   - Accepts two take descriptors (`takeA`, `takeB`), compares sample rate, bit depth, byte length, duration, and SHA-256 digests, and emits a formatted 7-bit ASCII comparison report to `cns00` (`>> [TIMBRE:COMPARE] ${takeA.id} vs ${takeB.id}`).
3. **`authorVoiceNotes.buzz.ts` (`authorVocalDirectionNotes`)**:
   - Validates that `noteText` matches `/^[\x20-\x7E\r\n\t]+$/` (rejecting non-ASCII input with `NON_ASCII_DIRECTION_NOTE_BREACH`), records the note on the target character profile in `voices/voice_manifest.json`, and logs `>> [VOICE:NOTES:SAVED]` to `cns00`.
4. **`demoteVoice.buzz.ts` (`demoteActiveVoiceVersion`)**:
   - Verifies that a fallback version (`targetFallbackVersion >= 1`) exists on disk in `voices/versions/`, repoints `voices/active/${characterSlug}.wav` and the manifest's active `reference_wav_path` to the fallback version without deleting the quarantined `.wav` in `voices/versions/`, and logs `>> [VOICE:DEMOTE]` to `cns00`. Rejects demotion when no prior fallback version exists (`VOICE_DEMOTION_NO_FALLBACK_BREACH`).
5. **`registerDialect.buzz.ts` (`registerDialectOverride`)**:
   - Validates `word`, `arpabet` (uppercase ARPAbet tokens with stress digits `0-2`), and `ipa` (pure 7-bit ASCII representation), writes the entry into `voices/lexicon.json`, and logs `>> [LEXICON:REGISTERED]` to `cns00`.
